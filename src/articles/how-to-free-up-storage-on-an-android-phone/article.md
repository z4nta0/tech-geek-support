When your Android phone runs out of storage, things start going wrong: photos won't save, apps won't update, and you may see a "storage full" or "running out of space" warning. The fix is to clear out things you don't need, and your phone has tools to help.

**Storage** is the space your phone uses to keep things: photos, videos, apps, and downloads. It isn't the same as the phone's memory, which is what running apps use. That's why closing apps won't free up storage.

Menus differ a little from brand to brand. Below, you'll find steps for Google Pixel and most other Android phones, plus steps for Samsung Galaxy phones.

> **Before you start:** Deleting photos and videos can't always be undone. Make sure anything precious is backed up (for example, in Google Photos) before you delete it.

## Step 1: See what's taking up space

**On a Google Pixel or most Android phones:**

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Storage**.
3. You'll see a list of categories, like Images, Videos, and Apps, with how much space each one uses.

**On a Samsung Galaxy phone:**

1. Open the **Settings** app.
2. Scroll down and tap **Device care**. (On some older Samsung phones, this is called **Battery and device care**.)
3. Tap **Storage**.

Start with whatever is biggest. For most people, that's photos and videos.

## Step 2: Remove photos and videos that are already backed up

If you use **Google Photos** and backup is turned on, your pictures are already saved safely online. You can remove the extra copies from the phone itself.

1. Open the **Google Photos** app.
2. Tap your profile picture or initial in the top corner.
3. Tap **Free up space on this device**.
4. Check how much space it will free, then tap the button that says **Free up** and the amount.

Your photos stay in Google Photos and you can still see them in the app when you're online. They just won't take up room on the phone anymore. Photos less than 30 days old may stay on the phone.

> **Tip:** This step only removes photos that are already backed up. If backup isn't turned on, nothing is removed by this tool, so turn backup on first.

## Step 3: Delete files you don't need

**On a Google Pixel or most Android phones:**

1. Open **Settings**, then **Storage**.
2. Tap **Free up space**.
3. Scroll to a category, tap **Select files**, pick what you don't need, and tap **Move files to Trash**.

**On a Samsung Galaxy phone:**

1. Open **Settings**, then **Device care**, then **Storage**.
2. Tap a category such as **Images**, **Videos**, **Audio files**, or **Documents**, and delete what you don't need.
3. Tap **Trash** to permanently remove files you deleted earlier. Deleted files keep using space until the trash is emptied.

> **Tip:** Your **Downloads** folder is often full of old attachments and forms you only needed once. It's a good place to look.

## Step 4: Remove apps you don't use

1. Open the **Play Store** app.
2. Tap your profile picture, then **Manage apps & device**.
3. Tap **Manage**, then check the box next to apps you no longer use.
4. Tap the trash can to uninstall them.

You can always reinstall an app later, and you won't pay again for an app you already bought.

On Samsung phones, **Device care**, then **Storage**, also has an **Unused apps** list showing apps you haven't opened in 30 days.

> **Tip:** Android can also **archive** apps you rarely use. Archiving removes most of the app but keeps your information and the icon, so it's quick to bring back. Turn this on in the **Play Store**: tap your profile picture, then **Settings**, then **General**, then **Automatically archive apps**.

## Step 5: Clean up downloaded music and videos

Downloaded shows, movies, and music for offline listening can be very large. Open the app you downloaded them in (such as YouTube Music or Netflix) and remove downloads you've finished with.

## If that didn't work

- **Turn on automatic cleanup (Pixel and some other phones).** Go to **Settings**, **Storage**, **Free up space**, tap the menu, then **Settings**, and turn on **Smart Storage**. It removes backed-up photos for you when space runs low.
- **Restart the phone.** It sometimes takes a restart for freed space to show up.
